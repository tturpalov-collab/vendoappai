Problem Terminals — August 2026

Eleven terminals show payment failure patterns that the rest of the fleet does not. This note records what the payment data shows, what can already be ruled out from it, and what needs to be checked on the platform side.

Window 1–25 Aug 2026 Attempts 77 370 Fleet CE rate 0.52 % (excl. terminal in §1) Payments analysed 535 236 since May 2025

§1Terminal that has never authorised a payment

Terminal 221400017033 has produced 792 payment attempts and 792 technical failures over its entire recorded history. Not a single approval, ever. In August alone it accounts for 176 of the 576 connection errors in the whole company — 31 %.

What the data rules out. A 100 % failure rate sustained across the entire lifetime is not radio coverage and not a flaky link — a weak signal produces an intermittent rate, not a constant one. Every failure carries response_code = 'CE', meaning the terminal never received a host response at all, so the issuer never saw these transactions.

Please check: whether this terminal ID is registered and routable on the acquiring host; whether its TID/MID pair was ever provisioned; and whether the terminal's host address configuration matches the working units of the same operator.

§4Data issues found while investigating

Four items below are not terminal faults but properties of the data. They affect anyone computing metrics from these tables, so they are worth fixing at the source.

4.1  Empty response code conflates two different events

Of 27 792 payments with an empty response_code, roughly 26 400 are not payment attempts at all. They come from an amusement-park site where visitors tap their own MiFare balance cards on the vending POS by mistake. The terminal correctly rejects a non-payment card, but the event is stored indistinguishably from a genuine host failure.

The signature is clean and does not depend on the operator:

pos_entry_mode = 'CONTACTLESS'      -- not 'EMV CONTACTLESS'
  AND coalesce(aid, '') = ''
  AND coalesce(pan, '') = ''         -- 26 065 rows, 98.7 % of all such events

Genuine card payments always carry EMV CONTACTLESS or QR CODE READ. A dedicated flag for “card is not a payment instrument” would remove the ambiguity. Until then, company approve rate reads about 3 points lower than reality.

4.2  vendotek_unit.last_seen_at is in seconds, not milliseconds

Unlike the millisecond epochs used elsewhere in the schema, this column stores epoch seconds. Dividing by 1000 yields January 1970 for 703 of 1 008 units. Worth either normalising the column or documenting it. The format of vendotek_module.status_changed_at has not been verified and may have the same issue.

4.3  A test terminal is live in production data

Terminal 221400017147, named IntersoftPOS Testing 5.4.2026, produced 6 367 payments totalling 3 654 AED — an average of 0.57 AED. These are test runs sitting in the same tables as real sales and inflating transaction counts. A test/production flag would be more reliable than encoding it in the location name.

4.4  Unexplained spike in empty response codes, June 2026

In June, 1 142 empty response codes originated outside the amusement-park site — against 1 in May, 121 in July and 105 in August. They are spread across every operator (modern-vending 399, tazizi 338, coffepoint 98, and so on), which points to a platform-level event rather than a customer-side issue. The peak was the week of 22 June, with 1 848 empty codes in seven days. No cause has been identified.

This is the one open question in the report. If anything was deployed, migrated or reconfigured in the week of 22 June 2026, that is the most likely place to look.

§6How these numbers were derived

So the figures can be reproduced or challenged.

-- payment amount (either column alone is zero for the other tender type)
amount = coalesce(cash_amount, 0) + coalesce(cashless_amount, 0)

-- operation time: pos_localtime_at, NOT synced_at
--   synced_at is the mirror export timestamp; every row falls in 19-25 Aug 2026

-- success: vendotek_payment.approved
-- technical failure (terminal got no usable host response):
response_code IN ('', 'CE', 'TO', '907', '909', '911')
-- issuer decline: everything else, chiefly '116' insufficient funds

-- joins are logical only; the schema declares no foreign keys
vendotek_payment.vend_id             -> vendotek_vend.id
vendotek_payment_cashless.payment_id -> vendotek_payment.id
vendotek_vend.unit_id                -> vendotek_unit.id

All figures exclude the MiFare mis-taps described in §4.1 unless stated otherwise. “Attempts” counts rows in vendotek_payment; “CE” counts response_code = 'CE' on the cashless detail row.

CE (communication error) is written by the terminal when the payment host does not respond within its timeout. The transaction does not reach the issuer, so neither the card nor the cardholder is involved in the failure.

Source: production payment tables, 535 236 payments from 6 May 2025 to 25 August 2026, currency AED. Terminal serial numbers are given as vendotek_unit.sn. Prepared for platform-side investigation; raw extracts for any line in this report are available on request.
